Smaller projects and coursework

Autonomous robot car
First year. OpenCV on a Raspberry Pi, with PID control on an ESP32 driving a servo to keep the car on a straight line.
Microstrip low-pass filter
3 GHz cutoff, designed and simulated in Keysight ADS. Compared stepped-impedance and Kuroda layouts: 38.4 mm against 55.8 mm, with the larger one rejecting the stopband better.
Active and digital filters
4th-order Butterworth Sallen-Key low-pass filter, cutoff measured at 133.88 Hz in LTspice against a 134 Hz target, plus a z-domain digital version with notch zeros.
Two-stage BJT amplifier
Biased for a 2 mA collector current with a predicted midband gain of 50.6, then reconciled against LTspice and re-verified on E-series values.
Self-attention text classifier
Self-attention built from first principles in PyTorch to classify factory maintenance reports. Tuning raised test accuracy from 70% to 95%.
UK day-ahead load forecasting
Ridge regression on a year of half-hourly grid demand, with regularisation picked by 5-fold cross-validation. Test MSE went from 900 to 5.6.
Digital modulation and BER
Bit error rate against Eb/N0 for QPSK through 256QAM over an AWGN channel in MATLAB and Simulink, checked against theory.
Enterprise network design
Eight VLAN segments, redundant Proxmox hosts, DNS, VoIP for 32 concurrent calls and a firewall rule set, costed at about £54.8k in year one.
Binary exploitation and threat modelling
A stack buffer overflow and return-to-libc exploit, analysed in Ghidra, and a STRIDE threat model for a healthcare app.
