Pulse oximeter

An optical SpO2 and heart rate measurement system, built end to end: the analogue front end, the firmware, the signal processing and the link to a PC.

Role
Solo or team? Add your role
Year
2024
Tools
STM32 (C and C++), on-chip ADC with DMA, FFT, RS-485, CPLD logic in Quartus Prime

What I built

  • Analogue front end. The signal chain for the photodiode, conditioning the optical signal before it reaches the ADC.
  • Firmware. STM32 firmware in C and C++, using the on-chip ADC with DMA for acquisition.
  • Signal processing. FFT-based processing to recover heart rate from the optical waveform.
  • Data link. An RS-485 transceiver link to send data from the board to a PC.
  • Logic. CPLD logic designed in Quartus Prime.
