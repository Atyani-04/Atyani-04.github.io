Formula Student: VCU firmware and 60 V indicator board

I was part of the University of Nottingham Formula Student team, working in both the vehicle control unit (VCU) software and hardware sub-teams. I wrote CAN bus firmware for the VCU and designed a safety-critical indicator board for the car.

Role
Member of the VCU software and hardware sub-teams
Year
2024 to 2025
Tools
STM32, STM32CubeIDE, CAN bus, PCB design, Formula Student rulebook

What I built

  • VCU firmware. STM32 firmware, written in STM32CubeIDE, that handles CAN bus messaging between the car's subsystems. It works to the team's message definitions and timing requirements.
  • 60 V indicator board. A safety-critical indicator circuit designed to Formula Student regulations. I designed the whole PCB myself, from schematic to layout, selected the components, checked the design against the rulebook and sent it for fabrication. The finished board was fitted to the competition car. Add one line on what the indicator shows and who it is for
